# Product (authenticated app) UX

- The `product-screen` skill is MANDATORY for any create/edit here — a `PreToolUse` hook (`.claude/hooks/product-screen-guard.mjs`) loads it automatically; you never wait to be asked.
- **Goal, not table**: design from the user's job and what success looks like. Never default to a CRUD table of rows — that shape is allowed only when the job really is scan/compare/manage many rows.
- **Never a blank screen**: every zero-data view uses `EmptyState` (icon + why + the ONE next action). A brand-new user must find a path to value (the aha moment).
- **Less text on the screens, and no empty space** (the owner, 2026-11-09): a page, a card or a section opens on its title, and what it is for sits behind the "i" beside it (`description` of `SectionTitle` and `ConsultationStepHeader`; a page's own `h1` takes an `InfoHint` beside it; 200 characters at most in every locale, kept by `src/lib/i18n/hint-budget.test.ts`). In sight under a title (`detail`) stay only state or data that tells the thing apart, and what must be read before acting: clinical safety (the anamnesis's "não informado", tongue and pulse being her observations, the receituário never from the AI, the acervo's purpose notice), a consequence (what each practice-scope or audio-retention choice changes, what a purchase or an export does), consent and legal notes, personal data, and the instructions on a patient's own page (`/consentir`, `/gravar`, `/documento`). A row that opens something shows its affordance, never a sentence saying so; a definition that repeats its title goes; a shortcut tile is its icon and its name (the home's row). A list or section with nothing in it takes a drawing of `@/icons/illustrations` (`EmptyState` `illustration`, `framed={false}` inside a card or the grid): the patients (accounts) and the archived ones (storage), a patient's first consultation (launch), the agenda and the home's week (platforms), the library chat, the acervo search and the protocols (documentation), the connected apps (integrate); a state of permission, of something unavailable or of a failed read keeps its icon. A once-only flow (`/onboarding`, the auth pages) keeps the line that says what a step needs before it is taken. The admin consoles (pt-BR in place, one reader) were not brought into this yet: a new console follows it.
- Long setups use `SetupWizard` (progressive disclosure); steps to first result use `OnboardingChecklist` + `@flyee/onboarding` (state only; step definitions live in the project's code).
- **Engagement**: completion drive (`ActivationProgress`) and competence feedback only. Never ship points, badges, leaderboards or streaks — documented failures, and streaks carry regulatory risk.
- MUI v9 + theme tokens only, next-intl for every string, inline form errors near the field, skeletons that reserve space.
- **Semantic fields**: phone, CPF/CNPJ, CEP and money never go in a raw `TextField` — use `components/product/fields/*` (mask + check-digit validation + CEP auto-fill via `@flyee/fields`); persist `onlyDigits()`, never the masked string.
